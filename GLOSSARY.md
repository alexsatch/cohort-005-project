# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase, and what that does to a learner's place in the course.

## Language

### Enrollment

**Enrollment**:
A learner's place in a course, which gives them access to its content. Their progress belongs to it.
_Avoid_: Access, Seat (for an individual learner), Subscription

**Suspended Enrollment**:
An Enrollment that no longer gives access to the course, because the purchase behind it was refunded. The learner's progress is kept, and the Enrollment resumes if they get the course again, whether by buying it or by claiming a team seat.
_Avoid_: Revoked, Deleted, Cancelled, Unenrolled

### Team Seats

**Team Seat**:
A paid place in a course that a team buys in bulk, for one learner to claim.
_Avoid_: Licence, Coupon (for the place itself)

**Coupon**:
The code a learner uses to claim a Team Seat.
_Avoid_: Voucher, Redemption code

**Voided Team Seat**:
An unclaimed Team Seat that can no longer be claimed, because the team purchase behind it was refunded.
_Avoid_: Cancelled seat, Expired coupon

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A buyer's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. The team approves or declines it. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team hasn't decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an admin has accepted. Approving it leads to a Refund, and suspends the Enrollment the purchase paid for or voids its unclaimed Team Seats.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an admin has turned down, with a reason the buyer is told. It has no Refund, and the buyer may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a buyer after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund the team has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the buyer.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed, and the team can try sending the money again.
